1. Mastery-Gated, Not Calendar-Rushed

  • No fake deadlines: Take 5 years or 15 years—advance ONLY after passing each Stage's Mastery Gate.

2. Builder-to-Breaker Advantage

  • Dev Superpower: Your Web, Mobile & Game dev background lets you write vulnerable & secure systems from scratch.
  • Direct Observation: Master primary truth tools (gdb, strace, readelf, tshark, curl) before any automated scanner.

3. Pure Fundamentals First, AI Later

  • AI as Mentor Only (Stages 0–6): Zero distracting LLM-wrapper projects early—build razor-sharp human intuition and pure C/Python/Go code tools.
  • AI Security Deep Dive (Stage 7): Study LLM/Agent security from first principles once you are already a solid systems engineer.
